How a world gets built.

01 · Listen

The brief.

Who the brand is, who it needs to reach, what it's launching, and what has to be true when it's over.

02 · Imagine

The world.

One big idea: a place, a story and a reason for people to care, whether they meet it in a room, on a screen or in their hands.

03 · Define

The rules.

Palette, language, materials, sound and style: the details that keep every touchpoint unmistakably part of the same world.

04 · Design

The pieces.

Everything the world is made of: campaign assets, product and merch, spaces, menus, content, and the partners who belong inside the story.

05 · Build

Make it real.

A launch, a shoot, a collection, an activation or a season-long series: produced, staffed and delivered, with the right people in it and the cameras in mind.

06 · Measure

The proof.

Reach, sales, sentiment and impact, reported back, so the world keeps working long after launch day.

Founder

Avery Jordan

A worldbuilder working across spirits, fashion and culture, who builds every project to serve the people inside it.

A brand and experiential marketer, Avery builds activations for Edmond's Honor Whiskey and has produced fundraisers for Emerging 100 of Atlanta drawing more than 2,100 guests, with partners including Aguasol, Tito's, Cutwater and Viarae. A Morehouse College graduate and MBA candidate in Product and Brand Management at Emory's Goizueta Business School, he founded Atlas to build brands as worlds people can step into.
