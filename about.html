Mission

To create culturally intentional experiences and brand activations that connect people, elevate environments, and shape how modern culture engages.

Vision

A globally recognized cultural studio that influences how modern brands, venues, and communities gather, connect, and experience culture.

Founder

Avery Jordan

Avery is a brand and experiential marketer working across premium spirits, fashion, and culture. He builds activations for Edmond's Honor Whiskey, has produced fundraisers for Emerging 100 of Atlanta drawing more than 1,950 guests with partners including Aguasol, Tito's, Cutwater, and Viarae, and is an MBA candidate in Product and Brand Management at Emory's Goizueta Business School. A Morehouse College graduate, he founded Atlas to build brands as worlds people can step into.
