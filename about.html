How a world gets built

From an idea to 1,250 guests.

One world, start to finish: Park 100, Oak Bluffs Edition.

  1. 01 · Imagine

    The world

    An idea from a meeting: one night in Oak Bluffs, Martha's Vineyard, without leaving Atlanta.

  2. 02 · Theme

    The rules

    A style guide and look book that told guests what to wear, and why the place matters.

  3. 03 · Assets

    The story, sold

    A rollout that sold the world before the gates opened: what came with every ticket, and where it would take you.

  4. 04 · Partners

    Brands inside the world

    Six sponsors, each built into the theme, like ViiV's wall of golf clubs, not hung on a banner.

  5. 05 · The room

    Built for real

    Arches, sail walls, Summer House and a putting green. Park Tavern became the Vineyard.

  6. 06 · Result

    It goes home with you

    1,250+ guests, 498,000 views, and $22,000+ in programming for Atlanta students.

Founder

Atlas is led by Avery Jordan, a worldbuilder working across spirits, fashion and culture, who builds every project to serve the people inside it.

A brand and experiential marketer, Avery builds activations for Edmond's Honor Whiskey and has produced fundraisers for Emerging 100 of Atlanta drawing more than 2,100 guests, with partners including Aguasol, Tito's, Cutwater and Viarae. A Morehouse College graduate and MBA candidate in Product and Brand Management at Emory's Goizueta Business School, he founded Atlas to build brands as worlds people can step into.
